# power-supply-mount Specification

## Purpose

The two brackets on the bottom beam rows and the power supply brick between them.

## Requirements
### Requirement: The brick hangs between the bottom beam rows
Two printed `power_supply` brackets SHALL clamp a 50.5 x 30.5 x 108 mm
power supply outside the rear-left bottom beams (between towers Z and
X), one screwed radially into the lower row's outer slot and one into
the upper row's, bolted to each other with two M3 screws and nuts, the
brick's 30.5 mm face spanning the two rows.

#### Scenario: The brick is held
- **WHEN** the brick is displaced 0.3 mm up, down or either way along the beam
- **THEN** it interferes with a bracket in each case, and displaced 0.05 mm it does not
